Camera mode (shot direction)

Stick bright tape (orange/green/pink) near the bat tip. Put the phone on a stand at the bowler's end, facing you, with your swing in view. Start the camera, tap the tape in the preview to lock its colour, then swing. While the camera is on it replaces the motion sensor for swing detection.
