How to use BatForm

Practice With Precision – everything you need to set up.

1. Host the page (one time)

  1. Upload index.html to GitHub Pages, Netlify or any HTTPS static host.
  2. Open the HTTPS link on your computer and on your phone.

2. Tape the bat (red tape)

  • Tape 1 – handle: wrap a 3–4 cm band of matte red tape fully around the handle, about 18 cm from the top.
  • Tape 2 – tip: wrap a second band around the blade, about 8 cm above the toe.
  • Two tapes give the app the bat's angle; with one tape it still follows your hands but the bat will not tilt.
  • The camera adjusts itself to the tape colour and the room light (and ignores big red areas like shirts or posters). If it ever picks the wrong thing, press Re-detect tape colour or tap the tape in the preview.

3. Pair the Screen and the phone

  1. On the computer: Screen → ⚙ Settings → Create pairing code. A 4-letter code, a QR code and a link appear.
  2. On the phone: scan the QR (it opens Bat sensor and connects by itself), or open the app → Bat sensor → type the code → Connect.
  3. Both sides show “connected ✓”.
Not connecting? Both devices need internet for the handshake. Create a new code and try again. If you open the file straight from your disk (not a web address) the QR cannot work – host it first.

4. Set up the camera

  1. Phone in landscape on a stand about 4 m in front of you (bowler's end), at about chest height, facing you.
  2. Bat sensor → Start camera. The red-tape preset is already selected; if the tape is not circled in the preview, tap it to lock its colour.
  3. Take your stance, then press Calibrate stance (or C on the computer). It also calibrates by itself when you stand still for a second.
  4. On the Screen, if the bat moves the wrong way, tick Mirror camera; if it moves too little or too much, change Camera reach.

5. Play

  1. Screen → ⚙ → Start session (or press Enter).
  2. The bowler runs in; you see the pitch as a batsman does. Swing when the ball arrives – the ball meets the bat you hold.
  3. After each ball: speed, variation, contact quality, shot played, runs, and any dismissal – including edges.
Keyboard test mode: Space = swing · ← off side · → leg side · ↑ lofted · ↓ defensive · C calibrate · F full screen.

Tips

  • Raise Ball slow-down while learning; set it to 1.0 for real speed.
  • Bat forgiveness makes contact easier while the camera is not perfect.
  • Pick a ball colour that stands out on your screen (red, white, pink, yellow).

Troubleshooting

  • Tape not found → brighter light, matte tape, tap the tape in the preview to re-lock the colour, raise colour tolerance.
  • Bat lags or jumps → steadier phone, more light (the camera needs fast shutter), landscape mode.
  • Motion sensor off → page must be HTTPS and permission granted.
